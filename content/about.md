## Background

I was born and raised in Arizona and received my undergraduate degree at [The Citadel](https://www.citadel.edu/), The Military College of South Carolina, where I graduated Cum Laude with a B.S. in Computer Science and a Minor in Management Information Systems in just three years.

After graduating, I returned to Arizona and earned my Masters of Science in Software Engineering from [Arizona State University](https://www.asu.edu/), with a focus on Distributed Systems.

## Career

In March 2019, I began my current role at [Quantum Metric](https://www.quantummetric.com/) in Denver, CO. I've been fortunate to work with incredibly talented engineers and innovative teams throughout my career, spanning companies like Carvana, CommercialTribe, and others across the Denver and Phoenix tech scenes.

I'm an enthusiastic engineer, leader and learner with a passion for professional and personal development. I believe that highly functioning organizations start at their foundation. Optimizing for transparency ensures teams and individuals possess a deep understanding of larger organizational initiatives. I care deeply about continuous improvement to how teams build, release and iterate on products they own.

## Personal

Outside of work, I live in Denver, CO with my wife Jaymie and our two daughters, Ryann and Logan. When I'm not writing code or leading teams, you can find me running, biking, snowboarding, hiking, watching baseball, or catching a movie.
